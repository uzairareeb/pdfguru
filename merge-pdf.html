Frequently asked questions

Is there a limit on how many PDFs I can merge?

There is no fixed limit. Very large files are only constrained by your device's memory, since everything runs locally in your browser.

Will merging change my page quality?

No. Pages are copied exactly as they are — text, images and layout stay untouched.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. PDFGuru processes everything on your own device, so your documents never leave your computer.
