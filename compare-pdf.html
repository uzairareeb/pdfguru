Frequently asked questions

What exactly is compared?

The text content of both PDFs, line by line. Layout-only changes (fonts, spacing) won't show as differences.

Do the PDFs need the same number of pages?

No. Added or removed pages simply show up as blocks of added or removed lines.

What about scanned PDFs?

Scans have no text layer to compare. Run them through our OCR tool first, then compare the results.
