Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the OCR?

Very good on clean, straight scans. Faded print, skewed pages or handwriting will lower accuracy.

Why is there a 10-page limit?

Recognition runs entirely on your device and is CPU-heavy. For longer documents, process in chunks.

Does the PDF still look like my scan?

Exactly — the original page images are kept, with an invisible searchable text layer added underneath.
