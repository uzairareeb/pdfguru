Frequently asked questions

Can I rotate just pages 2 to 5?

Yes — choose the page-range option and type 2-5.

I have three PDFs to rotate. Do I do them one by one?

No, drop them all in. Each rotated file comes back inside one ZIP.

Does rotation lower image quality?

No. Rotation only changes the page orientation flag and layout — content is not re-rendered.
