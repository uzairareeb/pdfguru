Frequently asked questions

How much smaller will my file get?

It depends on the file. PDFs with duplicated or unused objects shrink noticeably; already-optimized files may barely change. We always report the honest before-and-after sizes.

Does compression hurt quality?

No. This rebuild does not re-encode images or text, so visual quality is unchanged.

What metadata gets removed?

Title, author, subject, keywords and producer fields — handy when you want a clean file to share.
