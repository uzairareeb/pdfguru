About PDFGuru

PDFGuru is a free collection of online PDF tools: merge, split, compress, convert, edit, sign, watermark, OCR and more. Everything is designed to be simple — upload a file, pick your options, download the result.

Private by design

Most online PDF tools upload your documents to a server. PDFGuru works differently: every tool runs entirely in your browser, on your own device. Your files are never uploaded, stored or seen by anyone else — which also means there's no queue, no account and no waiting.

Free, no signup

All tools are free to use and need no account. We keep the lights on with advertising, so the tools themselves stay free for everyone.

What's next

We're steadily adding more tools — password protection, Office-to-PDF conversion, PDF/A archiving and translation are all on the roadmap. If a tool you need is missing, tell us via the contact page.
