Frequently asked questions

Is this proper legal redaction?

This v1 tool draws opaque boxes over content — fine for everyday privacy, but not certified redaction. For legal-grade removal, use dedicated desktop software.

Can I redact multiple pages?

Yes. Switch pages with the page selector and draw boxes on each before downloading.

I drew a box in the wrong place — can I undo?

Yes, the Undo button removes your last box. You can also reload the page to start over.
