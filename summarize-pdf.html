Frequently asked questions

How does the summary work?

It scores every sentence by how information-dense it is and keeps the top ones in their original order — an extractive summary, not an AI rewrite.

Is this as good as an AI summary?

It's a fast, private first pass. It surfaces key sentences faithfully but won't paraphrase or explain like a large AI model would.

Does it work on scanned PDFs?

Only if the PDF has a real text layer. For scans, run OCR first, then summarize.
