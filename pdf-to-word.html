Frequently asked questions

Will the Word file look exactly like my PDF?

Not exactly. This is a text-preserving conversion: the words come across faithfully, but complex layouts, tables and fancy formatting are simplified into clean flowing text.

What about scanned PDFs with no real text?

Scanned pages are just pictures of text. Run the file through our OCR tool first to get searchable text, then convert.

Which Word versions can open the file?

The DOCX format works in Microsoft Word 2007 and newer, Google Docs, LibreOffice and Apple Pages.
