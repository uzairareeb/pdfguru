Frequently asked questions

Will my tables keep their exact columns?

This is a text-based extraction, so it works best on simple layouts. Intricate tables may need a quick tidy-up in Excel afterwards.

What do I get for a 5-page PDF?

A workbook with five worksheets — Page 1 through Page 5.

My PDF is a scan. Will this work?

Scanned pages contain no real text. Run them through our OCR tool first to make the text readable, then convert.
