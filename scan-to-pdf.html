Frequently asked questions

How do I use my phone camera?

Tap the upload box on your phone — it offers the camera directly. Snap pages in order.

The scans aren't searchable text. Can I fix that?

Yes — run the resulting PDF through our OCR tool to add a searchable text layer.

Is there a page limit?

No fixed limit, but very large photo sets depend on your device's memory.
