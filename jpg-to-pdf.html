Frequently asked questions

Which image formats work?

JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and BMP. Anything your browser can display can become a PDF page.

How is the page order decided?

The order you add the files. Add them in the sequence you want the pages to follow.

Will my photos lose quality?

JPG photos are embedded as-is; other formats are converted losslessly to PNG first.
