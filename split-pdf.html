Frequently asked questions

How do I write page ranges?

Use dashes for a span and commas to separate groups, for example 1-3, 5, 8-10. Page 1 is the first page of the document.

What do I get when I split every page?

One PDF per page, bundled into a single ZIP download. For very long documents (over 60 pages) we ask you to use ranges instead.

Does splitting reduce quality?

No — pages are copied without re-rendering, so quality is identical to the original.
