Frequently asked questions

Can numbering start at a number other than 1?

Yes — set any start number, handy when your PDF is one chapter of a larger document.

What does “1 of 10” do?

It prints the current page and the total, like “3 of 10”.

Will numbers overlap my content?

They're placed at the margin you choose. If your pages are full-bleed, increase the margin value.
