Frequently asked questions

JPG or PNG — which should I pick?

JPG for scanned photos and smaller files; PNG for sharp text and graphics where clarity matters most.

What does quality change?

Higher quality renders each page at more pixels, so text stays sharp when zoomed — at the cost of larger image files.

Are the images searchable text?

No — they are pictures of your pages. If you need searchable text, try our OCR tool instead.
