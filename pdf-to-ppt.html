Frequently asked questions

Can I edit the text on the slides afterwards?

Slides are high-quality images of your pages, so text isn't editable as text boxes — but the layout is preserved pixel-perfectly.

What slide size is used?

Widescreen 16:9, the modern default. Each page image fills the slide.

Will animations or links survive?

No — this converts the visual pages only. It's ideal for presenting static documents.
